# AISIN ASK Preview 2 Design Direction

## Direction

**Precision Ring** uses the circular geometry of AISIN clutch, bearing and rotating-system components as the single recurring visual device. It appears as the hero aperture, product stage, image crop, launch field and footer close.

The hero pairs a deep AISIN Blue editorial panel with one dominant, authentic product photograph. Product discovery becomes an interactive circular stage with a compact category index. Subsequent sections shift naturally between an editorial catalogue, a dark corporate statement, a gold range-update band, a technical document index and a compact enquiry form.

## Reference translation

- SBD Automotive: disciplined corporate navigation, strong information hierarchy and clear expertise pathways.
- Toyota South Africa: high-confidence product storytelling, strong task access and image-led first screen.
- Q Refinish: sculptural dark product moments and purposeful section-to-section contrast.

No reference layout or brand expression has been copied.

## Brand system

- AISIN Blue: `#001A72`
- Support blue: `#08389A`
- AISIN Gold: `#B89B57`
- Main type family: Frutiger with system sans-serif fallbacks when Frutiger is unavailable locally
- Official supplied AISIN ASK logo and brand lockup used without redrawing

## UX and CX priorities

- Five-item primary navigation and one direct enquiry action
- Clear first-screen description and product route
- Keyboard- and touch-friendly product explorer
- Direct technical downloads and audience-specific paths
- Non-transmitting preview form with local validation and honest status messaging

## Content constraints

No company statistics, testimonials, event dates, contact details or legal information have been invented. Official public contact information, policy links, current stories and recipient integration remain required before launch.

## Preview 2 visual refresh

The supplied Frutiger regular and bold font files are self-hosted in `assets/fonts`. The desktop and mobile type scale and section spacing are compacted. Four generated editorial images provide a consistent automotive visual language; they depict generic scenes and components and must not be presented as photographs of AISIN products or facilities. The hero WebM is an eight-second camera pan derived from the generated road still, not recorded driving footage. The official AISIN ASK logo remains the supplied artwork.
